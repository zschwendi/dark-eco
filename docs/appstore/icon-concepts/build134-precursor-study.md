# Build 134 Precursor icon study

> **Status:** Candidate artwork only. The active app icon is unchanged.

The maintainer rejected the active Build 131 icon and preferred the prior icon's
stronger ancient-tech silhouette, while noting that neither clearly evokes the
Jak franchise. Follow-up direction made **Precursor technology** the primary
identity and reduced Dark Eco to a background accent.

References inspected:

- Active: `AppIcon-build131-iOS-1024.png`
- Prior: commit `ef0bed9774a725d6ea9261d5eed9f35dcbcc3841`,
  `AppIcon-iOS-1024.png`

The active icon collapses toward a generic purple battery at 32 points. The
prior icon keeps a stronger ancient-machine silhouette, but its complete radial
seal still reads as a generic sci-fi target.

## Candidate 1 — Precursor reliquary

`build134-precursor-reliquary-v1.png` proved the bronze, oxidation, green Eco,
and violet-shadow palette. It was rejected during internal review because its
tall silhouette reads as a throne or totem at 32 points.

## Candidate 2 — broken Precursor relay

`build134-precursor-relay-v2.png` is the recommended candidate for maintainer
review. Three large asymmetrical bronze plates cradle a green Eco heart without
closing into a full ring. Downscaled 32- and 64-point inspections retain the
gold mechanism, green center, and broken silhouette.

Final generation prompt:

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: original Apple app icon source for Eco
Primary request: Create a compact, unmistakably ancient Precursor-tech relay
icon. Precursor identity must dominate; use Dark Eco only in the background
shadows.
Scene/backdrop: full-bleed opaque square, near-black black-violet background
with a restrained soft vignette, no environment or floor.
Subject: one squat, near-circular but deliberately broken ancient mechanism
made from exactly three huge asymmetrical bronze-gold stone-metal plates
wrapping around one bright yellow-green Eco heart. The three plates form an
open, offset spiral or interrupted cradle with a large upper-left notch and a
smaller lower-right opening; they do not close into a full ring. The Eco heart
is one simple irregular rounded-diamond crystal at the center. A small bronze
base wedge is integrated into the bottom plate rather than forming a pedestal.
Style: restrained premium 3D game-object icon; chunky ancient carved metal and
stone, warm aged bronze, muted gold, sparse turquoise oxidation, vivid green
Eco core, and faint violet fill in the deepest gaps. Mysterious, colorful
PS2-era platform-adventure energy rather than sterile science fiction.
Composition: centered squat silhouette with only four major readable shapes,
broad negative space, slight three-quarter depth, and immediate 32-point
readability as a broken golden ancient-tech relay around a green Eco heart.
Constraints: original design only; no text, official glyph, rune, character,
creature, known collectible, exact power cell, copied asset, full circular
seal, radial target, compass, battery UI, hazard symbol, eye, military badge,
MAR shape, earthquake motif, lightning bolt, orange core, micro-circuitry,
watermark, or baked Apple mask.
```

Both candidates were generated with the built-in image-generation tool. They
contain no project or retail game assets. Do not place either in the active app
icon asset catalog until the maintainer selects a direction and its complete
Apple icon-size set passes visual review.
